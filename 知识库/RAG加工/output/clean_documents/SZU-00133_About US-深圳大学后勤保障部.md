# About US-深圳大学后勤保障部

In 1983, Shenzhen University was established with the establishment of the General Affairs Department, responsible for the school's logistics affairs. Later renamed as Life Service Headquarters, General Affairs Department, Logistics Department, Logistics Department, etc. In January 2021, the school merged the functions of the Logistics Department and the Infrastructure Department to establish the Logistics Support Department. The Logistics Support Department is fully responsible for the management functions of campus infrastructure, logistics services, and support on behalf of the school.
The Logistics Support Department focuses on the central task of the school's development, serves the teaching and research needs of the school, meets the requirements of teachers and students yearning for a better life, adheres to the original aspiration, bears the mission, strengthens supervision, and provides excellent services, providing strong support for the construction of high-level universities in the school. The Logistics Support Department adheres to the concept of "serving teachers and students, striving for excellence", and integrates "full staff education, full process education, and comprehensive education" into the logistics support work, striving to create "safe logistics, efficient logistics, green logistics, smart logistics, and clean logistics". The school is a national civilized campus, one of the top ten most beautiful campuses in China, a green school in Guangdong Province, and the first batch of water-saving universities in Guangdong Province.
